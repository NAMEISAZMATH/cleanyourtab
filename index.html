Prep Your Tablet

          Frequently Asked Questions
        

        

          Everything you need to know about vibe coding and tablet safety.
        

      


      

        
        

          
            Does this require rooting or unlocking the bootloader?
            
          
          

            No! It is 100% no-root. Everything runs via standard Android Debug Bridge (ADB) user-space commands (pm disable-user --user 0). System partitions remain completely untouched.
          

        


        

          
            Will this void my tablet's warranty?
            
          
          

            No. Enabling Developer Options and running ADB does not void manufacturer warranties. Since no bootloader unlocking or root exploit is used, Knox / warranty bit counters remain untouched at 0x0.
          

        


        

          
            How do I revert back to the stock factory UI?
            
          
          

            The AI automatically generates a restore_stock.sh script in Phase 1 before disabling a single app. To undo everything, simply run bash restore_stock.sh over ADB and your original stock launcher and all apps are restored immediately.
          

        


        

          
            Will this erase my personal photos or data?
            
          
          

            No. CleanYourTab does not perform a factory reset or touch user storage. Your photos, files, downloads, and personal apps remain completely intact.
          

        


        

          
            What if I want to customize the HUD colors or dock apps later?
            
          
          

            That's the beauty of Vibe Coding! Just open your Antigravity chat and tell the AI in plain English: "Change the HUD accent to Neon Purple" or "Add YouTube to dock slot 2". The AI will recompile and deploy your updates in seconds.
